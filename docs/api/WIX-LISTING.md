## Review notes (for the Wix reviewer)

```
OneShopLab audits a Wix store's product catalog (Wix Stores required) and rewrites titles, descriptions, alt text and photos with AI. Nothing is published to the store without the owner's approval. Every AI generation spends credits.

1. Install the app on a site with Wix Stores and a few products. The OneShopLab dashboard page opens.
2. Click "Create my workspace" (account created from the site owner's email, 150 free credits), or "Link to my account" with the demo account below.
3. The page shows the catalog score, synced products, pending changes and credits. "Sync now" pulls the products again.
4. "Open OneShopLab" opens the full app in the same dashboard page, limited to this site: open a product, click "Generate all", compare, then "Apply to my store" — the change reaches the Wix product within seconds.
5. Plans and packs: "Subscription" in the app header. Every purchase goes through Wix checkout (new tab); back in the app, the plan and credits update. While the app is unpublished Wix prices every plan at $0: those test purchases are capped at 500 credits per account and never refilled (the app shows this). To move to a lower plan, cancel in Wix first.
6. Removing the app ends the plan.

Demo account: appreview@oneshoplab.com (password given in the credentials field).
```
